## Rules for forks and redistributions

If you fork or redistribute this project:

1. Keep this notice and the non-official statement visible.
2. Do not present the fork as an official DeepSeek product.
3. Do not use the DeepSeek logo as your organization's or repository's avatar
   in a way that suggests affiliation.
4. If you use the project commercially, or at a scale where brand confusion is
   likely, obtain your own permission from the brand owner or replace the
   brand assets with your own.
5. If the rights holder asks for removal of brand assets, remove them promptly.

## Takedown readiness

Brand assets are intentionally grouped in a small number of files so they can
be replaced or removed without touching the application logic. The application
name and installer metadata should also be reviewed before any public release
that could be confused with an official DeepSeek product.
